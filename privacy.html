Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Byte Stories Studio is a private, self-hosted application used only by the owner of the Byte Stories YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on that channel. It is not available to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, add videos to playlists, read the upload status of the owner's videos, and read the owner's channel analytics.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored only on the owner's own computer, with the tokens encrypted. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and can be deleted from the app.
