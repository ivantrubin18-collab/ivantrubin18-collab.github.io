Bricked Era

Bricked Era produces original documentaries and short videos about forgotten technology, engineering and internet history. The project was previously named Byte Stories.

The owner uses a private, self-hosted production tool, previously named Byte Stories Studio, to prepare and publish original videos through the official YouTube Data API. The tool is not offered to other users.

The optional TikTok integration supports account authorization and posting selected original videos through TikTok's official APIs. It requires app access granted by TikTok and a review of the video and publishing choices before each upload. Public posting depends on TikTok approval; creating this website does not grant that approval.
